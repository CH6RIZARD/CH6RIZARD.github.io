Hugo's backpacks. Each file is cut the same way (static "pack", hinged "flap", markers "hinge"/"mouth"/"hole"),
recoloured toward a light matte look, scaled to 1 unit tall and meshopt-compressed. The .wasm copies are the same bytes.
Default; choose another with ?pack=voxel | quaternius-small | tawpshelf

  Front lid (tab, buckle, strap over it) hinged; body top under it opened into a mouth.

  "Hiking Backpack" by Voxel_dev, https://poly.pizza/m/pVuHdEBRUs. License: CC0 1.0.
